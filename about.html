About Qamar Stories

Qamar (قمر, "moon" in Arabic) shares short, gentle bedtime stories for children aged 3 to 8, in English and Arabic. Each story has a simple lesson about kindness, courage, honesty or sharing.

Stories are written with the help of AI (Claude, by Anthropic) and published automatically, one per night. Automatic checks run before a story is published, but no person reviews every story. Please read a story yourself before reading it to your child.
