About Qamar Stories

Qamar ("moon" in Arabic) shares short, gentle bedtime stories for children aged 3 to 8, in English and Arabic side by side. Each story has a simple lesson about kindness, courage, honesty or sharing.

Stories are created with the help of AI (Claude, by Anthropic) and published automatically. Please read a story yourself before reading it to your child.
